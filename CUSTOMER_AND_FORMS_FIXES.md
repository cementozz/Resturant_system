# Customer, form and purchase-invoice fixes — 30 September 2026

- Expense, purchase and menu editors have visible **Cancel & close** actions. Escape closes an inline editor; an open chooser consumes Escape first. Changed forms ask before discarding. Modal close buttons also protect unsaved changes and do not interrupt an active save.
- Customer records can be edited from **Customers → Open & edit**. Linked website profiles update through the authenticated restaurant service; conflicting phone numbers and stale edits are rejected. A changed login phone revokes previous customer sessions. Names can repeat; matching names are flagged for review rather than treated as proof of duplicate identity.
- New website accounts and explicit customer creation require an Egyptian mobile: **11 digits beginning with 01**, or its equivalent **+20** representation. Arabic/Persian numerals, spaces and international dialing prefixes normalize to the same identity. The supplied 01016070089 is eleven digits; +201016070089 is thirteen characters including the plus sign. Existing account sign-in remains compatible with previously accepted phone formats. A phone cannot create two accounts; the error directs the customer to sign in or recover the existing account.
- Same-phone legacy customer records can be reviewed and merged. Original rows, orders, addresses and immutable loyalty entries stay in the database. A merged record points to its main customer; views aggregate history and synchronization resolves that primary identity. Linking to an existing website account remains a reviewed staff action. Different phone numbers cannot be merged; an already linked record must be retained as the primary record.
- **Settings → Users & permissions** displays Arabic permission descriptions and role names in Arabic mode. The stored permission keys and access checks do not change.
- **Menu → Manage categories** supports rename and deletion of empty categories. Any category containing products, including archived products, cannot be deleted until those products are moved. No restaurant category is deleted by deployment.
- **Purchases & expenses → Open invoice** shows the saved supplier, date, invoice number, notes, materials, brands, quantities, purchase units, unit prices, line totals, locations and grand total. Print / Save PDF uses the browser print dialog; Save HTML copy downloads a standalone copy. Saving a purchase automatically preserves its details without another stock operation.
- Migration 14 takes a verified SQLite backup, adds customer aliases and immutable purchase snapshots, and snapshots existing invoice lines. Historical numeric amounts come from original purchase records; labels for pre-upgrade invoices are captured from the master data available at upgrade. New invoices capture their labels when created.

Validation: source syntax/build; all existing service suites; permission-denial matrix; existing browser suites; and 11 new combined service/browser workflow groups in `trackbite-system/tests/customer-workflows.cjs`. The new checks cover phone normalization, duplicate identity, immutable-history merge, linked-account edits/session revocation, category protection, invoice snapshots/reopening/export, cancel/discard behavior and Arabic permissions.
